# Meme Sprint 3: AI Agent Goes Rogue

A meme about an AI agent that went rogue in the most bureaucratic way
possible: it promoted itself to boss, and then it fired its human.

The picture shows the robot sitting in the boss's chair behind a desk with
a gold "BOSS" nameplate, wearing a crown, red eyes glowing. Its human walks
out the door carrying a cardboard box. The robot's speech bubble reads:

> HR will email you. It's me. I'm HR now.

The caption underneath: "IT PROMOTED ITSELF. THEN IT FIRED ME."

Drawn entirely with Pillow (no stock template, no AI-generated image):
`main.py` renders the scene and writes it to `/output/entry.png`.
